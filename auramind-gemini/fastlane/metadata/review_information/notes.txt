Reviewer notes for the App Review team.

To exercise AuraMind end-to-end during review:

1. On the Auth screen, tap "Continue with email" and create a developer test account with the dev-reviewer email pattern. New accounts fall under the 14-day "Starter" trial that unlocks all features (no payment card required). To inspect the signed-in state, ignore the Marketing splash and go straight to /auth.

2. Once signed in, the Dashboard appears. Tap "Generate Deck" → enter "Spanish vocabulary" → wait ~10s → the AI generates a fresh 10-card deck. Tap the deck → "Start Session" → answer each card using the Again / Hard / Good / Easy ratings. The FSRS scheduler rebalances the deck immediately on each rating.

3. The streak widget, achievements, and study-stats all update live — no sign-out needed. To inspect micro-permission usage, see Privacy & Permissions below.

Privacy & permissions

• Microphone access (NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription + NSMicrophoneUsageDescription) is OPT-IN and only requested when the user taps "Voice Mode" while reading a card. No background audio capture.

• Camera access is not requested.

• Local notifications are opt-in ("Daily Reminder" toggled in Settings).

• Crash reporting is collected via Sentry (sentry.io) under our corporate scope. Crash events are anonymised before egress; PII is never sent.

• No advertising SDKs ship in this binary. No third-party tracking.

TestFlight internal testing has been completed prior to submission. Logs from internal runs are available at the App Store Connect build details page.
